While working on this assignment, I used ChatGPT to create an AI image of a plate of waffles with syrup. 
I wanted to use an image of waffles because they are my favorite breakfast meal. 
If I could, I would have them every morning before work. Even though I love waffles, I didn't have a photo of waffles in my camera roll, so I decided to use it for the generative AI image. 
For some reason, I didn't have high hopes that ChatGPT could produce a good image. I was expecting one that you could tell was an AI-generated image. 
All I asked ChatGPT was "create a photo of waffles with syrup," and it gave me a somewhat realistic image on the first try. 
I was impressed and went with that one. It reminds me of a waffle commercial or one of those Pinterest or Instagram photos you see of people posting online of their perfect breakfast.
I am not a big fan of AI, especially videos or "AI art," so I had my actual, real photos of my favorite foods chase around the AI-generated waffles since it's not real like them.
