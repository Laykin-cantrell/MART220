When comparing my p5.js sketch to the version generated by ChatGPT, they look similar at a glance due to the moon being in a similar position, but looking at it more, you can tell which one was generated through ChatGPT. My version feels more visually planned out, as I spent time adjusting where I want the moon and stars placed and how many mountains I wanted. I also added color to my ground to make it look like grass, creating a stronger contrast between the ground and the night sky. The ChatGPT version appears darker and has no contrast. It got the idea of a starry night with mountains in the background, but it feels more like a rough draft of the layout than a finished version. It placed the stars randomly, the mountains aren't evenly across the page, and the colors are so dark you can't tell the ground very well or where the front of the mountain ends. 
The difference shows how people and AI in general approach creative work differently. While AI focuses on quickly creating something that technically fits the prompt, I was more focused on how my layout actually looked. My version looks more stylized and colorful than the ChatGPT version. While working on the project, it made me think more about spacing, shapes, and colors, rather than relying on an AI-generated answer to do it for me. I had fun with this project, as it helped me see how creative coding isn't just about writing code, but also about making artistic decisions on our projects.
