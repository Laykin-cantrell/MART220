For my final prokect, I exoanded my earlier pigeon game into a more complete interactive game. The player controls the good pigeon and has to defeat the dark boss pigeon before time runs out.

How to Play!

-Use WASD to move the good pigeon.
-Press SPACE to shoot projectiles/
-Avoid the oranges because they lower health.
-Can hide behind the block obstacles for cover.
-Collect the lemons to gain points.
-Defeat the dark boss pigeon before the timer reaches 0 or before you lose all of your health.
