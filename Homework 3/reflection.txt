For this assignment, I built on my last assignment and made it interactive. When you hold down the
spacebar, the stars twinkle until you let go of the spacebar. I also made the moon move when it's
clicked on. It moves back and forth across the sky, and you can click it again for it to stop moving.
I had a very hard time figuring out how to create the twinkle effect, but in the end, I'm glad it worked out.
Adding those features made the design feel more alive and interactive, instead of looking like a
Microsoft Paint photo. For the ChatGPT version, I had ChatGPT add onto its version and create the 
same interactives as I did. It's not interactive at all. The moon is in a constant loop, and you can't
click on it to stop or start moving. Along with that, there are no stars in the sky anymore. 
With no stars in the sky, you can't hold down the spacebar to make them twinkle. It looks like AI couldn't 
comprehend how to create an interactive design.
